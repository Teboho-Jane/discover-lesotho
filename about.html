About Lesotho

Discover the Mountain Kingdom, its people, culture and natural beauty.

The Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom surrounded by South Africa. It is known for its breathtaking mountains, valleys, rivers and unique landscapes.

The country has a rich history and culture that makes it a special destination for visitors who want to experience the beauty and traditions of Southern Africa.

From spectacular waterfalls to historical sites and mountain reserves, Lesotho offers many places for tourists to explore.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Beautiful Mountains

Explore breathtaking mountain landscapes, valleys and natural scenery across Lesotho.

Rich Culture

Experience Basotho culture, traditions, history and the welcoming people of Lesotho.

Adventure

Discover exciting destinations and outdoor experiences throughout the Mountain Kingdom.
